# Joma Portfolio Rebrand — AI Automation & Systems Builder

## Rebrand Objective
Reposition the portfolio from a broad Web, Design & Automation identity into a clearer current identity: **AI Automation & Systems Builder**.

The portfolio should communicate that Joma can translate a business problem into a working digital system using AI, automation, APIs, data, UX, frontend development, debugging, validation, and documentation.

The existing web/design and IT background remains visible as a differentiator, but supports the current systems-builder identity.

## New Positioning
**Primary title:** AI Automation & Systems Builder

**Supporting statement:** I design and build practical digital systems that connect AI, APIs, workflows, data, and people — from business requirements and architecture through implementation, debugging, validation, and documentation.

**Personal methodology:** BE EXCITED. BUILD. BREAK THINGS. DEBUG. LEARN.

## Design Archive
Keep the older web work and label it clearly as **Design Archive · Earlier Career**. It documents the design and production foundation that continues to influence the modern systems work.

## Video / Motion Portfolio
Keep the video work. Position it as **Motion & Visual Systems** or **Video & Multimedia Archive**. It demonstrates the creative side of the same person who now builds technical systems.

## About
Suggested theme: **A designer who learned to think like a systems builder.**

Explain the progression honestly:
Web and visual design foundation → IT and systems experience → long break → return through practical experimentation → AI-assisted development → automation → agents → systems integration.

Avoid positioning primarily as an “AI Prompt Specialist.” Prompting is a skill used inside the larger systems-building capability.

## Visual Direction
Technical but human. Clean typography. Strong workflow diagrams. Code/data details. Subtle motion. Interactive project demonstrations. Before/after debugging stories.

Avoid the generic “AI consultant” look. The personality should communicate: **Builder + Designer + Experimenter**.

## Rebrand Success Criteria
A visitor should understand within 10 seconds:
1. Joma builds AI-powered automations and digital systems.
2. Joma understands APIs, workflows, data, UX, and implementation.
3. Joma learns by building and debugging real experiments.
4. Joma has a design/IT foundation that makes the profile multidisciplinary.
5. The work is practical and hands-on.
